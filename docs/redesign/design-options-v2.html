IDistinguishR redesign, round 2: three modern layouts

Round 1 only changed colours inside the same boxed, centred layout, which is why it still felt vintage. These three change the structure itself: how the page is organised, where the search lives, and how instructors are shown. All text uses your real wording; the instructor names are from your demo data and the ratings and prices are illustrative. Every colour pair is measured against WCAG 2.2 AA.

1. Cobalt Stage2. Midnight Studio3. Friendly Colour Blocks

1. Cobalt Stage

Bold and confident. One saturated cobalt field owns the top of every page; everything else is clean white. The search bar overlaps the hero edge so the main action is the first thing you reach.

Layout: Full-width colour hero with huge type and a tall photo; floating search bar straddling the hero edge; a row of instrument tiles; instructor cards with a big price.
Type: Hanken Grotesk, one family from 400 to 800 weight

2. Midnight Studio

Dark, focused and a little theatrical, like a practice room after hours. A bento layout gives each job its own tile: search, availability, instruments, and the photo.

Layout: Asymmetric bento grid instead of a hero banner; a stacked search tile; a tile that shows real availability slots; horizontal instructor rows.
Type: Schibsted Grotesk (headings and text)

3. Friendly Colour Blocks

Light, playful and very readable. Each instrument owns a colour that follows it everywhere (tiles, cards, filters), and the whole search is one rounded bar.

Layout: Centred headline over a single pill-shaped search bar; large colour-block instrument tiles; instructor cards with a colour header that matches their instrument.
Type: Lexend (headings) + Atkinson Hyperlegible Next (text), both designed for readability
