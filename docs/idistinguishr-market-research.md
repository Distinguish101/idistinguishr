# European Software Engineering Job Market — Research Summary (2026)

Research pulled to inform the IDistinguishR stack decision, given the goal of using this project to strengthen a CV for Junior Software Engineer / Junior Software Developer / AI Engineer roles in Europe.

---

## Language & stack demand

Python has the highest raw job listing volume of any language across European tech postings in 2026, driven by the continued expansion of ML/AI engineering roles. The split is roughly 40% ML/data engineering and 60% backend. Companies hiring senior Python engineers in Europe include Booking.com, Adyen, and Netflix in Amsterdam; DeepMind, Google, and Palantir in London; and SAP and Zalando in Berlin.

## Full-stack role requirements

Most full-stack job descriptions at this level require proficiency in React, Node.js, Python, PostgreSQL, and cloud platforms like AWS. Full-stack developers qualify for a wide range of roles — from Software Engineer to Technical Lead — across fintech, healthtech, edtech, e-commerce, and enterprise IT, which are among the strongest hiring sectors in 2026.

## AI tooling and the junior hiring landscape

62% of developers now use AI tools (nearly 50% daily), boosting productivity by an estimated 35% — but this has also reduced junior hiring by roughly 25% at Big Tech. New graduates now make up only 7% of Big Tech hires, down from 32% in 2019, with hiring focus shifting toward senior developers who can validate AI-generated output.

The top four in-demand technical profiles in 2026 are:
1. ML Engineers (TensorFlow, PyTorch)
2. DevOps Engineers (Infrastructure as Code, CI/CD)
3. Full-Stack Developers (React/Node.js)
4. Backend Engineers (Python/Java)

## What this means in practice

- A real, well-built, end-to-end project matters *more* for junior candidates right now, not less — since junior hiring has tightened and AI-tool fluency is now assumed rather than a differentiator on its own.
- React/Node.js and Python both show up consistently across postings, but for different reasons: React/Node.js is the default full-stack combo, while a large share of Python's volume is specifically ML/AI-driven rather than general backend work.
- This supports building the MVP core in a fast, modern full-stack combo (React/Node.js/TypeScript + PostgreSQL) and adding a deliberate, scoped Python/AI component later — rather than committing to Python as the primary backend language from day one.
